# Gym strength training evidence register

| Source | Population and limits | Planning use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1. Currier et al. (2026), [ACSM position stand on resistance training prescription](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000003897) | Overview of reviews in healthy adults, with varied programs and training experience. The synthesis concerns adaptations across repeated training. | Match loading, volume and exercise choice to the athlete's goal and equipment. Consider heavier loads for strength and accumulated work for hypertrophy when history supports them. |
| S2. Currier et al. (2023), [Resistance training prescription network meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37414459/) | Randomized trials in healthy adults comparing combinations of load, sets and weekly frequency. Protocol rankings are group estimates. | Use goal-specific load and set choices informed by prior performance. Avoid treating the highest-ranked protocol as a requirement for every session. |
| S3. Grgic et al. (2022), [Repetition failure versus non-failure review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2021.01.007) | Meta-analysis of strength and hypertrophy outcomes, primarily in young adults and with varied protocols. | Failure is optional. Use a repeatable submaximal effort cue and adjust based on recent recovery and performance. |
