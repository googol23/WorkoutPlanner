# Running evidence register

| ID and source | Population and evidence limits | Planning use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1. Esteve-Lanao et al. (2007), [Impact of training intensity distribution on performance in endurance athletes](https://doi.org/10.1519/R-19725.1) | Small controlled study in trained endurance runners. Compared training programs over time, using physiological thresholds to define intensity. | Favor substantial easy work across training when relevant. Decide whether the next session needs intensity from recent training and the athlete's goal. Avoid turning the study's allocation into a personal quota. |
| R2. Soligard et al. (2016), [IOC consensus on load in sport and risk of injury](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2016-096581) | Multisport consensus with varied evidence and a focus on athletes. Individual injury risk remains uncertain. | Review external workload and internal response together. Consider recent changes in duration, intensity and frequency alongside non-training stress, fatigue and constraints. |
| R3. Saw, Main and Gastin (2016), [Monitoring the athlete training response](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2015-094758) | Systematic review across athlete populations, measures and sports. Individual self-report measures varied. | Give fatigue, stress and recovery reports meaningful weight when choosing the next session, even when device metrics appear favorable. |
| R4. Damsted et al. (2018), [Changes in training load and running-related injuries](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30534459/) | Running-specific systematic review with four eligible studies, heterogeneous load measures and limited evidence. | Avoid prescribing a universal weekly increase or estimating injury odds from an exported activity history. |
| G1. Garmin, [Understanding and customizing heart rate zones](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=w8qlOr7BQ16Z82HVrevpw9) | Device support documentation. Settings can depend on the device, sport profile and configured zone basis. | Interpret zone times with the athlete's actual settings. Require a supported basis before translating a zone label into a numerical target. |
