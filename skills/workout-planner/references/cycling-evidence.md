# Cycling evidence register

| Source | Population and limits | Planning use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1. Seiler et al. (2013), [Adaptations to aerobic interval training](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0838.2011.01351.x) | Seven-week comparison of effort-matched interval programs in trained recreational cyclists. It compared specific work durations. | Specify the duration and intensity of work together. Scale the amount of work and recovery to recent tolerated efforts instead of copying a study protocol. |
| C2. Stöggl and Sperlich (2014), [Polarized training in endurance athletes](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2014.00033) | Short-term intervention in trained athletes from several endurance sports, including cycling. This does not establish a universal intensity distribution. | Consider where a hard ride fits with easy volume and recent workload. Avoid extrapolating a group training distribution to a single ride. |
